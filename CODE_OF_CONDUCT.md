# Code of Conduct

Voxora follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and critique code rather than people.
Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or any other
project space.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the email on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/ParshvCrafts). Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer may remove content and block participants who break these rules.
